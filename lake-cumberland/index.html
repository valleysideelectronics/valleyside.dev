Lake Cumberland: Boat & Ramps

Coming to Google Play and the App Store.

Lake level, usable ramps, routes over water, and help on Lake Cumberland, KY.

Everything you check before and during a day on Lake Cumberland, in one app built by a local.

Lake level and dam releases

Today's pool elevation at Wolf Creek Dam, how it compares to last year, and whether they're generating.

Ramps that work today

Every public ramp, with whether it's usable at the current lake level, plus aerial photos so you know what you're pulling into.

Routes over water

Pick a marina, cove, waterfall or ramp and get a route that follows the lake's actual shape and keeps clear of the banks, including the arms other maps leave out.

Spots worth the trip

Coves, waterfalls, cliffs and swimming holes, with how far away each one is.

Hazards other boaters report

Stumps, rocks, shoals and floating debris, marked by the people who found them, with the lake level they were seen at and whether others confirm them. Report one yourself in two taps; no signal? It sends when you're back in range.

Events on the lake

Live music, poker runs and tournaments at the docks and marinas.

Help when something goes wrong

Emergency numbers and towing, saved on your phone so they work with no signal. Carry your towing membership card, and captains can check it right in the app, even out of range. Send a float plan to someone on shore before you go.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location stays on your phone unless you choose to share a hazard report.

Routes are a guide to which way to go. They don't know depth, buoys or other boats, so always keep a lookout and follow posted markers.
