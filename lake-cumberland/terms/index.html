Using the app on the water

The app helps you find ramps, marinas and spots, see the lake level and weather, and plan a way across the lake. It is a guide, not a navigation instrument.

  • Routes don't know depth, stumps, buoys, no-wake zones or other boats. The shoreline moves with the lake level. Always keep a lookout, follow posted markers and navigation rules, and use your own judgment.
  • Information can be out of date or wrong. Lake levels, releases, forecasts and gauges come from government sources and can be hours old. Ramp, marina and event details come from public sources, venues and other boaters. Check with the source when it matters.
  • In an emergency, call 911. Don't rely on the app to get help.

You are responsible for your boat, your passengers and your safety.
